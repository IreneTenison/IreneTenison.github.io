Irene Tenison

I am a PhD candidate at MIT CSAIL, advised by Dr. Lalana Kagal. My research focuses on memory- and compute-efficient post-training and inference methods for language and vision models, including on-device and federated learning.

During my PhD, I have been fortunate to conduct research at IBM Research's MIT-IBM Computing Research Lab, Nokia Bell Labs at Cambridge, UK, and Apple Inc. Before joining MIT, I completed my master's degree at Mila in Montreal, where I was advised by Prof. Irina Rish and Prof. Eugene Belilovsky.

My research goal is to make AI systems practical by developing methods that enable models to learn, adapt, and operate efficiently under real-world memory, computation, communication, and privacy constraints.
